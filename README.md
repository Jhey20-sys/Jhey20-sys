## 👋 About Me

I'm a 3rd-year **BS Computer Science** student at the **University of Science and Technology of Southern Philippines – Cagayan de Oro**. I work mostly on the **backend**: database schemas, row-level security, service layers, and serverless functions on **Supabase**. Alongside that, I do **computational linguistics research** on Philippine languages, especially Cebuano and Tagalog.

I like problems where the answer has to be both correct and secure, and I don't mind the long debugging sessions it takes to get there.
